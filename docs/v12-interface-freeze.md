## Termination protocol version 2

New StopMessage.message_type is WORKFLOW_STOP_REQUESTED/STOP_ACK, with protocol_version=2. The request event records required_roles; ACK follows completed cancellation. The graph records exactly one final NORMAL_TERMINATION or RUNTIME_FAILURE and identical TerminationResult in state/report before REPORT. The optional field preserves historical replay compatibility. TerminationResult fields are protocol_version, reason, outcome, failure_stage, stop_completed and runtime_failure. DIAGNOSIS_READY distinguishes resolved diagnosis with remediation disabled; STOP_TIMEOUT distinguishes incomplete shutdown.

PLAN_FAILED is an orderly business failure with workflow_status=FAILED and runtime_failure=False; budgets, unrecoverable errors and stop timeouts are execution failures. No duplicate STOP event is emitted. Read-only legacy normalization and all scoring policy live in evaluation/termination.py and evaluation/metrics.py, shared by both evaluation scripts, analysis and offline rescore. Call-level MODEL_FAILURE retains stage/call/task/attempt identifiers; MODEL_RECOVERED explicitly links recovery and DIAGNOSIS_ACCEPTED records accepted root/evidence plus safely bypassed specialist calls.
